TELUGU PRONUNCIATION PILOT - RECORDINGS REQUIRED

No human recordings have been supplied or reviewed. All 11 manifest entries
are pending. No pronunciation button is enabled for pending recordings.

Record one item per file, matching assets/data/pronunciation_pack.json.
Say only the Telugu item, not its English translation or a carrier sentence.
Use a natural, clear teaching pace, without exaggerated extra vowels.
Preserve short/long vowel distinctions and complete compound consonants.
For letter-chi, say the displayed syllable, not the bare consonant cha.

Deliver uncompressed PCM WAV, preferably mono 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz, 16-bit.
Keep a small quiet lead-in/tail; avoid long silence, clipping, background
music, filters and audible room noise. Use a consistent voice and level.
Do not record children for this pilot. Only use recordings with permission
to redistribute in this app; do not copy clips from another Telugu app.

An adult native Telugu speaker must listen to EACH final exported file and
confirm it matches the written item and is suitable for young learners.
Do not mark AI output or unreviewed recordings as human-reviewed.

Activation checklist for each supplied file:
1. Match the filename and exact Telugu text in the manifest.
2. Verify pronunciation, clarity and actual offline playback.
3. Confirm distribution permission and document the license/permission terms.
4. Change status to "reviewed", add nativeSpeakerReviewed: true,
   distributionPermission: true, and a nonempty license string.
5. Run the pronunciation tests, full suite and Android build.
6. Play the installed APK in airplane mode, replay rapidly, leave the question
   and background the app. Confirm stopping and no reward/progress changes.

The app validates approval metadata and WAV availability, not whether a human
actually reviewed a recording. Human sign-off remains mandatory.
